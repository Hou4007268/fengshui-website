🔮 Zi Wei Dou Shu Chart

Zi Wei Dou Shu (Purple Star Astrology) is a traditional Chinese astrology system that maps your birth time onto a 12-palace star chart. Each palace reveals insights about your personality, career, wealth, and relationships.

What is Zi Wei Dou Shu?

Zi Wei Dou Shu uses your birth date, time, and gender to generate a star chart with 12 palaces:

  • Life Palace (命宫) — Core personality and life direction
  • Career Palace (官禄宫) — Career path and work aptitude
  • Wealth Palace (财帛宫) — Financial fortune and money management
  • Marriage Palace (夫妻宫) — Love life and partner compatibility
  • Travel Palace (迁移宫) — Social life and opportunities abroad
  • And 7 more palaces...

How does it differ from Western astrology?

While Western astrology focuses on sun signs and planetary positions, Zi Wei Dou Shu uses a different system based on Chinese star positions. It provides a more detailed personality and destiny analysis through 14 major stars (like Zi Wei, Tian Ji, Tai Yang) placed across 12 life-area palaces.
