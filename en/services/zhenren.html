FAQ

What should I prepare before consultation?

Please prepare birth date/time (if available), address, floor plan or interior photos, and a clear issue description. Better input leads to better results.

How long does one consultation take?

Basic sessions are usually around 30 minutes, and deep analysis sessions around 60 minutes. Timing can vary by case complexity.

When will I receive the report?

Basic consultation gives direct verbal guidance. Deep analysis reports are usually delivered within 3 business days in PDF format.

What if I am not satisfied with the consultation outcome?

You can apply for one free supplementary session within 7 days. We will re-evaluate your case to ensure closure on key issues.
