FAQ

Is this service online or offline?

Both are available. Remote review is faster and cost-effective; on-site review provides deeper and more precise assessment.

What information is needed?

Usually address, floor plan, indoor/outdoor photos, and business type. Operator profile details improve precision.

How long before adjustments show effect?

Simple adjustments may show within 1-3 months; larger renovation-related changes may take 3-6 months.

Which industries are supported?

Applicable to retail, food service, beauty, education, clinics, finance, and office-based businesses with industry-specific focus.
