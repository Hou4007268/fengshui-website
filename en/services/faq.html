Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers before your consultation

What is the consultation process?
Step 1: Submit your request (problem description + basic info) → Step 2: Analysis (based on floor plan/photos/birth chart) → Step 3: Report delivery (1-3 business days) → Step 4: Follow-up review (weekly progress check)
What's the difference between remote and on-site service?
Remote consultation is done via WeChat voice/video, suitable for most feng shui issues (floor plan analysis, wealth adjustment, relationship consulting). On-site service requires the master to visit in person, suitable for large commercial projects or burial site selection. Additional travel fees apply for on-site service.
What's the difference between AI and live consultation?
AI Free Consultation is for quick basic questions. AI Deep Analysis (¥99) provides a detailed report. 1-on-1 Live Consultation (¥999) is with a senior feng shui master for personalized advice on complex issues.
Will an unfavorable door direction cause financial problems?
Not necessarily. Direction is just one factor. More important are the entry flow path, lighting, and storage. We recommend starting with entry cleanup and lighting optimization, then observing for 7-14 days.
With a limited budget, where should I start?
Focus on the 1-2 highest-impact issues: cluttered entryway, unstable bedroom sleep area, or kitchen door-stove alignment. Stabilizing these key areas is usually more effective than a complete overhaul.
Is feng shui optimization worth it for renters?
Yes. Renters benefit from reversible, low-cost adjustments like flow path cleanup, layered lighting, soft partitions, and storage optimization. Quick to implement, low cost to experiment.
How often should I review after adjustments?
We recommend a review every 7-14 days. Track changes in sleep, mood, and efficiency, then decide on next micro-adjustments. Avoid changing too many things at once.
Can I get a refund?
AI Deep Analysis: full refund before report delivery. 1-on-1 Consultation: full refund before appointment confirmation. No refunds after confirmation. Contact us via WeChat for special circumstances.
Do you serve overseas clients?
Yes. We serve Chinese-speaking clients worldwide via WeChat voice/video. We can schedule consultations according to your timezone.
