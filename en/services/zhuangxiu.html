FAQ

When is the best time to start renovation?

After planning is clear and key conflicts are resolved. For scheduling details, use practical constraints plus timing recommendations.

How should colors be chosen?

Color should balance direction, function, and daily comfort. Avoid one-rule-fits-all choices.

What are common renovation feng shui mistakes?

Common issues include direct door conflicts, stove-water clashes, mirror misplacement, congested flow, and weak ventilation/light planning.

Can I still improve feng shui after renovation is complete?

Yes. Furniture, lighting, zone definition, and usage habits can still produce meaningful improvement without major demolition.
