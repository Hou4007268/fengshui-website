FAQ

How is date selection determined?

We use a combined framework of traditional timing rules and personal profile matching, instead of generic calendar lookup.

What information do I need to provide?

Usually profile basics, event type, preferred date range, and location orientation if relevant. More detail improves precision.

Are online auspicious-calendar dates enough?

Public calendar dates are generic and not personalized. Professional selection adjusts dates to your specific profile and scenario.

What if my selected date becomes unavailable?

We provide backup options in advance. If none remain usable, we can recalculate based on your updated constraints.
