FAQ

How accurate is wealth analysis?

It is a decision-support framework based on traditional logic and practical interpretation. Final outcomes still depend on execution quality.

What information is required?

Basic input: birth date/time, gender, and birthplace. Extra context such as address, office photos, and role can improve precision.

How fast will I get results?

AI quick analysis is instant. AI deep package is usually generated within ~30 minutes after submission. 1:1 written report is usually delivered within 3 business days after consultation.

Do I need to buy feng shui products after analysis?

No mandatory product purchase. In most cases, layout and behavior adjustments are prioritized first.
