Stove Direction Selection Guide

Published: 2026-03-17 · Topic: Kitchen setup

Choosing stove direction is not about one absolute rule. Prioritize workflow, ventilation, and long-term usability first.

Diagnose first, then act

Check movement path, prep-cook-clean sequence, and heat/smoke handling before deciding orientation.

3-step execution

  • Step 1: Record kitchen friction points in daily use.
  • Step 2: Adjust one variable (stove facing, prep zone, or ventilation).
  • Step 3: Review weekly and refine gradually.

FAQ

Do I need full renovation?
Not always. Start with functional direction and local adjustments.

Can rentals optimize this?
Yes, with reversible changes in placement and workflow.

How long to evaluate?
Usually 1-2 weeks of consistent cooking use.
