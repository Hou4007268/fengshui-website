Through-Hall Wind Flow: Practical Fixes

Published: 2026-03-17 · Topic: Flow and zoning

Strong straight-through airflow often weakens comfort and staying quality. Segment the path and add pause zones first.

Diagnose first, then act

Identify direct visual and airflow penetration, then modify one variable at a time.

3-step execution

  • Step 1: Track discomfort by location and time.
  • Step 2: Add one boundary element (furniture, rug, or screen).
  • Step 3: Review weekly and refine.

FAQ

Do I need a hard partition?
Not necessarily. Start with soft zoning and flow breaks.

Can small apartments handle this?
Yes. Focus on reducing direct penetration and adding pause points.

When can I evaluate results?
Usually after around one week.
